Finance · Legacy modernisation

Reporting Database Change Facility

Designed and digitally transform legacy app systems for major finance and asset management giant in US.

ClientAEGON Asset Management (RDCF)
RoleVisual Design, Responsive HTML Design
Duration8 weeks
TeamPM, Technical Architects, Sr. Jr. Atos Dev Team, Testing Team, Business Stakeholders, Marketing Team, Client
ToolsDrawiO, VS Code

Challenge & Scope

The legacy application, which was 20 years overdue for an update, necessitated the use of multiple applications to access customer reports. This reliance on manual processes increased the risk of human error and data inconsistencies, making the task cumbersome and time-consuming for account managers. The primary challenge involved designing and developing five applications within a six-month timeframe, all while adhering to stringent data security and access restrictions. For security reasons, the client insisted on using only default Bootstrap classes, avoiding any third-party plugins or code.

The Solution

Re-designed the application flow by eliminating redundant data and unnecessary processes, based on high-level discussions and client interactions. Created dashboard UIs tailored for super admin, admin, and account manager roles, and developed responsive HTML designs with default classes. Simplified complex forms into intuitive designs and crafted responsive tables with advanced features. Established a reusable template for consistent UI across five applications, enhancing brand cohesion. Final design assets were handed over to development teams for implementation.

Achievement

After several feedback interactions with client and several iterations, the client finally used my HTML designs as a standard brand usage guidelines for rest of their internal applications. Our team bagged two more application revamps post delivering the 5 application designs.

Rapid Prototyping

Did a quick round of brainstorming session to understand the expected system behaviour, business goal and end-user task-flow tailored for multiple user roles.
