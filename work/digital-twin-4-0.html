Digital Twin 4.0

Design the digital twin 4.0 solution that caters various sectors and creates a digital clone of physical assets and their processes.

ClientJoint collab between SIEMENS & Atos
RoleUI Design/ UX Designer responsible for Research, Data Analysis, Interface Design and Prototyping
Duration13 months
TeamSIEMENS and ATOS Research & Development Team
ToolsMendix (Siemens proprietary design tool), Adobe Photoshop, Google Search, MS PowerPoint, MS Teams, InDesign

Project Overview

This project is in continuation of the R&D project of SIEMENS and Atos to build a digital twin 4.0 service that caters various domain industries within Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Hydraulics, Thermo-dynamics, Acoustics, and Medical.

The digital twins are used across sectors such as, aerospace, healthcare, oil & gas, renewable energy, additive manufacturing, mining, water, transportation, power generation, maritime, power distribution etc. which helps them track their assets health, performance in real-time. The DT users keeps a track of all their assets via DT Fabric app as it helps them with critical decision making that helps their teams to perform well within accepted timelines. It helps continually working on enhancing their knowledge fabric by gaining an insight about their users, track their locations and continually collect data usage statistics to gather data and generate history logs of physical products within the virtual space.

It helps domain experts investigate data patterns through complex AI algorithms, and ML that pull out error data, analyse it and quickly perform predictive analysis of spares that need precautionary measures and maintenance, helping them perform routine checks of all their physical assets and schedule timely maintenance for a smooth operation.

UX Solution

Participated in daily research activity, cocreation workshop, card-sort activity, affinity diagram mapping, to understand the business users and their needs. Crafted demographic charts that covered large user groups from novice to experts and highly expert groups. Crafted out personas based on data collected through card-sort activity. Designed multiple dashboard UIs that display KPIs based on target user needs and KPIs with access control from a security aspect. Made multiple design iterations

Graphic and Print Design Support

Designed an 8 page brochure, aligned with Atos brand guidelines for a European energy summit in 2018.
