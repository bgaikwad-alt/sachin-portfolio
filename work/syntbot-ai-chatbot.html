SyntBot AI Chatbot

Designed an AI assistant chatbot UI for internal use

ClientPhilips
RoleUX Design
Duration1 month
TeamATOS Syntel R&D Team, PM, Product Owner, Client Stakeholder, Technical Architects, BA and Marketing teams
ToolsInDesign, Teams, Daily Scrums, MS Office, Adobe Illustrator

Scope

The existing AI chatbot, developed for the client, required a redesign. The current version was overdue for a design update and lacked a human touch, leading to excessive clicks and resulting in employee disengagement.

The Solution

Redesigned a virtual AI assistant chatbot user interface to align with Atos brand guidelines and enhance usability. This was achieved by applying Gestalt principles such as connection, color, space, and similarity to effectively group categories. Graphical icon elements were incorporated to improve user-friendliness and intuitiveness. Atos branding was consistently applied throughout the flow to maintain brand consistency.

Designed two options: one that aligned with the client's brand guidelines and one for Atos Syntel, an internal user-facing interface.
