RESCA

Re-designed Remote Windfarm Monitoring, O&M System. Participated in ethnographic research, user interviews and mapped the research finding data into final designs.

ClientRENOM in partnership with ATOS
RoleUX Research/ Site Canvasing/ User Interviews/ UX Lead/ Visual Design/ Interaction Design
Duration7 months
TeamATOS and RENOM Research & Development
ToolsAdobe Photoshop, Balsamic, Google map, GPS Visualizer, Qliksense BI Dashboard, Qlikview, Any Desk, Site Visit, MS Teams, Skype, MS Office, and Adobe Illustrator

Challenge & Scope

The previous application contained an excess of information that was redundant for site engineers and technicians. It was primarily designed with a focus on business needs rather than the requirements of end users. As a result, site engineers and technicians relied on outdated processes, such as legacy tools and manual methods like pen and paper. Additionally, the absence of real-time tracking was due to missing data from the SCADA system. The solution also lacked a minimalistic and intuitive user interface, leading to inefficient reporting, delayed response times, and high maintenance costs.

The Approach

Engaged in field observation and conducted interviews with three target user groups: stakeholders, site engineers, and investors. Following the research analysis, developed user journey maps and created high-level personas for the target user groups. Mapped workflows by comparing data from previous applications to identify missing critical information, which facilitated the creation of the Daily Analysis Report (DGR) and enabled the viewing of essential information.

Our Solution

The newly designed solution offers an intuitive and minimalistic interface, prominently displaying critical KPIs on the main dashboard. It caters to three major user groups: site engineers, clients (investors), and admin users (management). The custom dashboard allows site engineers to efficiently perform routine tasks, enabling them to proactively address issues and conduct maintenance activities in advance. This results in reduced maintenance costs, decreased downtime, seamless execution, and 24/7 uninterrupted grid availability without any stoppage or outage.
